Privacy Policy - Ward AfterShift Upload

Last updated: 11 November 2026

What the app does

Ward AfterShift Upload is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Ward AfterShift. It uploads video files from the owner's computer to that channel through the YouTube API Services.

Data it accesses

  • With the youtube.upload permission it uploads videos, with their title, description, tags and privacy setting, to the signed-in channel.
  • With the youtube.readonly permission it reads the signed-in channel's name, only to check that uploads go to the right channel.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token and the list of uploaded video IDs are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's, nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and no data about other YouTube users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
